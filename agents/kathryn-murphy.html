Frequently Asked Questions

What types of properties are available on Realtyr?

Realtyr features a wide range of properties, including single-family homes, apartments, condos, townhouses, luxury villas, vacation homes, and commercial properties.

How do I search for properties on Realtyr?

You can use our advanced search tools to filter properties based on location, price range, property type, and more. You can also take compare listings to make informed decisions.

What kind of customer support does Realtyr offer?

Realtyr offers 24/7 customer support. Whether you have questions about listings, bidding, or using the platform, our support team is always available to assist you.

How can I get personalized property recommendations?

By creating an account and setting your preferences, Realtyr will provide you with tailored property suggestions based on your search history, saved listings, and location preferences.

How do virtual tours work?

Our virtual tours allow you to explore properties from anywhere. You can view high-quality 3D images, & get a real feel for the property without needing to visit in person.

How secure are my transactions on Realtyr?

We take security seriously. All transactions are encrypted, and we follow best practices to ensure your personal and financial information remains safe throughout the elling process.
